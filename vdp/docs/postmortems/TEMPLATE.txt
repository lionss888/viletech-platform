Postmortem template (unexpected red on main)

Copy this file to vdp/docs/postmortems/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.txt and fill every section.
After the write-up, add exactly one prevention item to an open plan or a new slice plan. Do not close with only a hotfix.

Date
Author
Status Draft or Resolved
Impact (roles, alpha, lead time of customer requests)

Executive Summary
What failed in product language. What customers or roles could not do.

Timeline (timezone)
First signal. Wrong ready claims if any. Root cause found. Fix landed. Prevention landed.

Root Cause
Primary cause. Contributing factors (local gate != required check, env mismatch, stale E2E, cancel-in-progress, secrets).

Action Items Immediate
What was fixed in the same day.

Prevention Measures
What lasting check, rule, test, or Callout was added so the class cannot silently return.

Lessons Learned
What DoD or Local QG button was skipped. What gate name should have been in the plan.

Sign-off
Author. Reviewer. Date.

Rule of use
Unexpected red on main or required GitHub check after a ready claim always gets a postmortem in this folder plus one prevention item. Expected red during an open known slice does not need a full postmortem.
